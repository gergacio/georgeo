Object

Chapter 12

Regulation 120.1

BS 7671 contains requirements for the design, erection and verification of electrical installations so as to provide for safety and proper functioning for the intended use.

Section 131 - Protection for Safety

-

Regulation 131.1 - General

BS 7671 is intended to provide for the safety of persons, livestock and property against dangers and damage which may arise in the reasonable use of electrical installations

Risk of injuries may result from shock currents excessive temperature, overvoltage

Section 132 - Design

-

Regulation 132.1 - General

The electrical installation shall be designed to provide for: i. The protection of persons, livestock and property in accordance with Section 131. ii. The proper functioning of the electrical installation for the intended use.

-

Regulation 132.16 – Additions and Alterations

“the rating & condition of any existing equipment, including that of the distributor, will be adequate, for the altered circumstances” and “earthing and bonding arrangements, must also be adequate for the safety of the addition or alteration”.
